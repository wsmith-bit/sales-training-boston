What Businesses Own and Control

After completing a sales training engagement, businesses should expect to own the following:

  • Customized Training Materials: All training documents, presentations, and tools developed during the engagement will belong to the business, allowing them to use these resources for future training and onboarding.
  • Sales Methodologies: The specific sales frameworks and methodologies developed during training sessions are owned by the business, ensuring they can consistently apply these strategies across their sales teams.
  • CRM Integration: Tools and processes for integrating sales strategies with existing CRM systems will be fully vested in the business. This includes any custom workflows or reporting tools created during the training.
  • Performance Metrics: Ownership of systems for tracking sales performance and accountability, especially metrics tailored to their specific needs, will be retained by the business for ongoing monitoring and adjustments.
  • Ongoing Support and Coaching Plans: If included in the engagement, any post-training coaching plans or support structures will remain under the control of the business.

What Remains with the Provider

While businesses gain ownership of customized materials and processes, it's important to recognize that some aspects may remain with the provider. These may include:

  • Proprietary Training Techniques: General training techniques and methods that are part of the provider's established curriculum may not be transferable.
  • Branding and Marketing Materials: Any branded materials that are part of the provider's standard offerings are likely to remain the intellectual property of the training provider.

Negotiating Ownership Language Upfront

To ensure clarity regarding ownership, it's critical to negotiate the terms of intellectual property rights at the beginning of the engagement. Here are a few steps to consider:

  • Define Deliverables: Clearly outline what materials and methodologies will be delivered and who will own them.
  • Discuss Customization: Ensure that any customized training content is explicitly stated as owned by your business.
  • Include Post-Training Support: Negotiate terms for any ongoing support, coaching, or licensing of materials that may be necessary for long-term success.
