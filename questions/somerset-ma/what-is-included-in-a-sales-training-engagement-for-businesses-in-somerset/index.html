Core Deliverables

When you engage with a sales training provider like Sales Training Boston, here’s what you can typically expect:

  • Customized Training Programs: Each engagement begins with an assessment of your current sales processes, pain points, and team dynamics. This leads to a personalized training curriculum that addresses your specific challenges.
  • Live Coaching Sessions: Participants benefit from interactive coaching sessions that provide real-time feedback and practical advice on improving sales techniques, objection handling, and closing strategies.
  • Accountability Systems: A key part of the training includes implementing accountability measures to ensure that the learned strategies are being adopted and practiced consistently across the team.
  • CRM Integration: Many training programs include assistance with adopting and utilizing CRM systems effectively, ensuring that your team is equipped to manage leads and customer relationships efficiently.
  • Sales Leadership Development: For those in leadership roles, there’s often a focus on developing coaching skills to foster a high-performance culture within the sales team.

Typical Scope Boundaries

It's essential to clarify what the engagement typically covers to avoid misunderstandings:

  • Duration: Most training programs span several weeks to a few months, depending on the depth and complexity of the training.
  • Follow-up Support: While initial training sessions are included, ongoing support may be offered at an additional cost, which can help reinforce learning and address new challenges.
  • Materials and Resources: Participants usually receive training materials, but any additional customized resources may incur extra charges.

What Falls Outside the Standard Agreement

Understanding what is not typically included can help you scope correctly:

  • Long-Term Consulting: While training is comprehensive, long-term strategic consulting or business advisory services may require a separate agreement.
  • Additional Personnel Training: Training for additional staff members not included in the original scope may incur additional costs.

Scoping Correctly from the Start

To set clear expectations from the outset, it’s crucial to have an open discussion with your training provider about your specific needs. Here are some tips:

  • Define Objectives: Clearly outline what you hope to achieve from the training. This could be increasing close rates, improving the consistency of your pipeline, or enhancing team confidence.
  • Identify Key Metrics: Agree on how success will be measured post-training to ensure accountability and track progress.
  • Discuss Budget: Be upfront about your budget constraints to help the provider tailor their offerings accordingly.
