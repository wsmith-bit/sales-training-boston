  • Sales Process Documentation: A detailed overview of the standardized sales processes implemented during training is delivered. This document serves as a reference guide for sales teams, ensuring everyone follows the same methodology to improve efficiency and accountability.
  • Performance Metrics: Providers typically establish key performance indicators (KPIs) at the beginning of the engagement.
