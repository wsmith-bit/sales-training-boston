What Remains with the Provider

While local businesses gain ownership of many valuable assets, certain elements may remain with the training provider, such as:

  • General Market Research: Insights and research that are used to inform the training but are not customized for the business may also remain with the provider.

Negotiating Ownership Language Upfront

To ensure clarity regarding ownership of assets post-engagement, businesses in Raynham should negotiate ownership language upfront. This involves:

  • Defining Deliverables: Clearly outline what deliverables will be provided by the training agency and ensure that ownership is transferred to the business.
  • Intellectual Property Clauses: Include clauses that protect the business's rights to any customized materials created during the training.
  • Reviewing Contracts: Before signing, businesses should carefully review all contractual agreements to ensure that they fully understand which assets they will control and which remain with the provider.
