What Remains with the Provider

While companies gain significant ownership over the customized assets, it’s important to recognize what remains under the control of the training provider:

  • General Training Frameworks: While specific content is customized, the overarching training frameworks and methodologies may be retained by the provider for use with other clients.
  • Intellectual Property Rights: Providers typically retain rights to their proprietary training materials unless otherwise negotiated. This includes any unique frameworks or tools that are part of their broader offering.

Negotiating Ownership Language

To secure ownership of all desired assets, it’s crucial for businesses in Kingston to negotiate clear ownership language in their contracts with training providers. Here are key points to consider:

  • Ownership Clauses: Specify which materials and processes will be exclusively owned by your company, and ensure this is explicitly stated in the contract.
  • Usage Rights: Define how the training materials can be used in the future, including whether they can be adapted or modified without additional permissions.
  • Retention of Rights: Understand which rights the provider retains and discuss potential licensing agreements if necessary.
