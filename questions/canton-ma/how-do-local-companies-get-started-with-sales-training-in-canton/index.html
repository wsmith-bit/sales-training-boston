First Steps After Signing

Once you choose a training provider, the initial step after signing the contract is to schedule a kickoff meeting. This meeting serves as the foundation for the program, allowing both parties to align on goals, expectations, and timelines. During this session, businesses in Canton can share specific sales challenges they face, from inconsistent pipelines to weak follow-up processes. This information is critical as it shapes the training modules that will be developed.

Information Local Companies Need to Prepare

To make the most of this kickoff meeting, companies should prepare relevant data, including:

  • Sales Performance Metrics: Current close rates, lead conversion ratios, and any historical sales data.

By compiling this information, businesses will enable the training provider to create a more effective and targeted training program.

Initial Discovery or Intake Process

Following the kickoff meeting, an in-depth discovery or intake process takes place. For instance, a company in Canton that specializes in B2B services might find that their sales reps struggle with objection handling or following up on leads, which are critical areas to address in the training.
