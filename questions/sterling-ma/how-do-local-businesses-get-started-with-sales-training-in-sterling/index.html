First Steps After Signing

Once you've signed a contract for sales training, the next steps typically involve scheduling an initial kickoff meeting. This meeting is crucial as it sets the tone for the training program and outlines expectations. During this session, you will discuss specific goals, challenges, and desired outcomes with the training provider. It's also an opportunity to introduce key team members who will be involved in the training process, fostering a collaborative environment from the outset.

Information Local Businesses Need to Prepare

To make the most of the initial meetings, it's essential for local businesses to prepare relevant information. This includes:

  • Current Sales Metrics: Gather data on sales performance, pipeline status, and conversion rates.
  • Team Structure: Provide an overview of your sales team, including roles and experiences.
  • Challenges Faced: Clearly articulate the specific obstacles your team encounters, whether that's inconsistent follow-up or weak objection handling.
  • CRM and Tools: Share information about the sales tools and CRM systems currently in use, such as Salesforce or HubSpot.

Preparing this information not only helps the training provider tailor their approach but also encourages a more focused discussion during the kickoff meeting.

The Initial Discovery or Intake Process

Following the kickoff meeting, the training provider will conduct a discovery or intake process. This typically involves a series of interviews and assessments with your sales team to identify individual strengths and weaknesses. In Sterling, where local businesses may vary significantly—from home services to B2B solutions—this process is vital for customizing the training program. The focus will be on understanding the existing sales processes and aligning them with best practices to ensure that your team can operate more effectively.

How Long Before Productive Work Begins

Businesses can expect to see initial results within a few weeks of engaging in training. Typically, the training provider will outline a phased approach that includes initial workshops, live coaching sessions, and accountability check-ins. Depending on the complexity of your needs and the size of your team, the entire process can take anywhere from one to three months before you start observing measurable improvements in sales performance.
