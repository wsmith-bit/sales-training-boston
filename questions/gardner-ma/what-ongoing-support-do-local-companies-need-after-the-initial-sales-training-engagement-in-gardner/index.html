What Deteriorates Without Maintenance?

Sales training is not a one-time event; it's the beginning of an ongoing journey. After the initial training, several elements may deteriorate if not actively maintained:

  • Skill Application: Sales skills, like any other skill, require practice to stay sharp. Without regular reinforcement, sales reps may revert to old habits, resulting in lost deals and missed opportunities.
  • Standardized Processes: The framework established during training needs to be consistently applied. If sales processes are not adhered to, you risk creating a disjointed approach that confuses both your team and your clients.
  • Accountability: In the absence of regular check-ins and performance evaluations, accountability can slip. This lack of oversight can lead to weaker sales pipelines and lower overall performance.

Some aspects of ongoing support can be managed internally, especially for businesses in Gardner that are resourceful and motivated:
